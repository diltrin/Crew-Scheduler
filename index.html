Drag a job card onto a calendar date to schedule it — the job stays in the Jobs box so you can schedule it again on other dates. Drag a crew chip onto a job already on the calendar to assign that crew to that specific booking (a different crew can be assigned if the job runs multiple dates). Drag the little handles on the left/right edge of a scheduled job to stretch it across several days, or drag the middle of it to move it. Saturdays and Sundays are greyed out by default — check the box in the corner of a weekend date to turn it on.
